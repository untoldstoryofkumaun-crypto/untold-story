Voices from the Himalayas

A community-centered storytelling and cultural preservation initiative documenting the unheard narratives of Kumaon's mountain villages, before they fade from memory.

What are we looking for

Stories that may never make a headline, but mean everything to someone

  • An old man's memories
  • A village that is slowly changing
  • A tradition that is disappearing
  • A woman's unheard story
  • A home left behind because of migration
  • Or simply the everyday life of people in the mountains
How it began

A journey that started with listening

My name is Sachin Garkoti, and I belong to Garkot village in Champawat district of Uttarakhand. I am a fellow of the 3rd batch of the Himal Prakriti Fellowship, and have been actively involved in community work, storytelling, youth engagement, and creative documentation in rural Himalayan regions.

I strongly believe that meaningful social change begins with change within ourselves. With this belief, I started engaging with villages and local communities to understand their realities, stories, struggles, and cultural experiences more closely. During this journey, I realized that many stories from our mountains remain unheard and undocumented — stories connected to migration, disappearing traditions, oral histories, women's experiences, local wisdom, cultural identity, and the everyday realities of village life.

This realization became the foundation of Untold Stories of Kumaon: Voices from the Himalayas — an effort to document and preserve the voices and realities of Himalayan communities through photography, visual storytelling, oral histories, written narratives, and creative documentation, so that local culture and human experience can reach future generations.

Why this project exists

Vision & mission

Vision

To preserve the voices, memories, culture, and realities of Himalayan communities through stories and photographs — and to build awareness, dialogue, and deeper connections between people, culture, and future generations.

Mission

To document untold stories and changing realities of mountain communities through storytelling and photography, preserving cultural heritage, highlighting unheard voices, and encouraging dialogue and community connection.

What we're working toward

Objectives & goals

Community empowerment & awareness

Strengthen rural communities by documenting local realities, amplifying community voices, and creating awareness around issues that matter to the village.

Preservation of culture & heritage

Protect and document the stories, traditions, and knowledge of the community so they remain alive for future generations.

Growth through creative storytelling

Use photography and storytelling as tools for personal learning, creative expression, and building meaningful social impact.

Proposed activity

How the documentation happens

Community story collection & oral histories

Document untold stories, lived experiences, and local knowledge through field visits and conversations with community members.

Photography & cultural documentation

Capture visual stories on village life, migration, traditions, and social realities through photography.

Youth & community storytelling engagement

Facilitate storytelling spaces and involve youth and local people in sharing and shaping their own narratives.

Digital archiving & public story sharing

Create an archive of stories and share selected narratives through public platforms to raise awareness and preserve heritage.

Project timeline

October 2026 — March 2028

Oct – Dec 2026

Field visits and community mapping in selected villages; building relationships and identifying stories and participants; initial documentation of oral histories and photo narratives.

Jan – Mar 2027

Continued story collection and photography; documentation of cultural practices, migration stories, and local voices; storytelling circles with youth and elders.

Apr – Jun 2027

Curation and organization of collected stories and visual materials; development of a digital archive and thematic photo stories; sharing initial stories through online/public platforms.

Jul – Sep 2027

Further field documentation with a deeper focus on social-issue stories; community engagement activities and youth participation; expansion of the archive and outreach.

Oct – Dec 2027

Public sharing of curated stories through exhibitions and storytelling forums; reflection with communities and feedback collection; strengthening documentation of cultural heritage.

Jan – Mar 2028

Final documentation phase and compilation of stories; review of project outcomes and impact; preparing to sustain and expand Untold Stories as a long-term platform.
